## 2. What we process

**When you play, even without an account (guest):**

- **An account identifier**: a random id created when you first open the game. No name, no
  email is asked.
- **Your games**: for each question, the operation shown, your answer, whether it was right,
  how long you took (in milliseconds), when you answered, whether you typed on the on-screen
  keypad or a keyboard, and the level you were playing at; for each game, its settings, the
  version of the game you used and when it was played.
- **Your levels and statistics**, computed from your games.
- **Checks on your games**: the server replays every game to verify it. A game that fails
  the check is refused and kept with the reason; a game with implausible timing may be
  flagged for review (it is never refused for that alone).

**If you save your progress (link an email):**

- **Your email address** and the **player name** you choose. Your player name and levels
  appear on the leaderboard.

**Only if you do it:**

- **Feedback** you send from the game (your message, with the version of the game).
- **Name reports** you make about another player's name.
- **During the invite-only beta:** the invitation code you entered.

**Technical data:** the hosting providers below receive your IP address and browser
information with every request and may keep it in their logs for security and operation.
The game itself does not record your IP address.

We do **not** use advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking cookies, and we do not sell
or share your data.

## 7. Your rights

You can **access**, **correct**, **delete**, **export** (portability) your data, **object** to
processing based on legitimate interest, and **restrict** processing.

- **Delete everything yourself, at once:** in the game, Profile → **"Delete my data"** (or
  "Delete my account"). Your account and all data stored about you are removed from the
  servers and from your device immediately.
- For any other request, write to **[[CONTACT EMAIL]]**; we answer within one month.

You may also lodge a complaint with the French data-protection authority, the **CNIL**
(www.cnil.fr), or the authority of your country.
